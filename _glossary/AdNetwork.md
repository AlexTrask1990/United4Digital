## Ad Network

An ad network serves as a vital intermediary in online advertising, linking advertisers seeking valuable impressions with publishers possessing ad space. Initially used primarily for surplus inventory, ad networks now play an integral role in the expansive programmatic advertising landscape, facilitating efficient matches between ad supply and demand.

Publishers integrate their chosen ad networks, while advertisers utilize these platforms to set up campaigns tailored to their specifications, including budget, targeting criteria, and frequency caps. Ad networks then identify suitable publishers for the ads, effectively bringing together both parties.

##### There are several types of ad networks available to cater to diverse needs:

1.	Horizontal ad networks provide extensive reach across a wide inventory, appealing to advertisers aiming for broad exposure.
2.	Premium ad networks focus on top-tier publishers, offering exclusive access to high-quality inventory.
3.	Specialized ad networks concentrate on specific types of inventories, such as video or mobile formats.
4.	Vertical ad networks connect niche advertisers with relevant publishers, ensuring targeted ad placement to specific audiences.

Benefits for publishers include rapid income generation and expanded audience reach, enhancing monetization opportunities. Ad networks also diversify ad creatives, increasing potential earnings by serving well-targeted ads. For advertisers, ad networks broaden access to diverse publisher inventories, optimizing audience reach within budget constraints and improving return on investment (ROI) through precise ad placement.

Ad networks operate manually, distinct from the automated programmatic advertising ecosystem that utilizes real-time bidding (RTB), demand-side platforms (DSPs), supply-side platforms (SSPs), and data management platforms (DMPs). 
While ad exchanges offer transparent and efficient ad buying, ad networks continue to thrive by curating ad inventory manually, albeit potentially facing obsolescence as programmatic advertising advances.

To select the right ad network, consider factors such as network size, format variety, and compatibility with RTB capabilities. 
Notable ad networks for publishers include Google AdSense, Facebook Ads, and Media.net, while advertisers may favor platforms like Apple Search Ads and TikTok Ads for effective ad campaigns.

In summary, while ad networks provide essential mediation between publishers and advertisers, their manual operation contrasts with the automated efficiency of programmatic advertising. Publishers and advertisers alike benefit from the tailored ad placements and revenue opportunities facilitated by ad networks, underscoring their continued relevance in the evolving digital advertising landscape.
